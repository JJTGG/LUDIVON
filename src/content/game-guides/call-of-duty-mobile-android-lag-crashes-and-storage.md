# COD Mobile on Android: Fix Lag, FPS Drops, Crashes and Storage Problems

Call of Duty: Mobile combines competitive multiplayer, battle royale and several other game modes in one mobile shooter.

But running the game comfortably depends on more than installing it successfully.

A phone may meet the game's minimum compatibility requirements and still struggle with demanding graphics settings. Another device may run gameplay smoothly but experience connection problems or crashes after an update.

These issues have different causes.

Before changing your settings or reinstalling the game, identify what is happening and follow a troubleshooting process that addresses the actual problem.

## 1. Identify the problem first

Start by looking at the symptoms.

| What you notice | Possible cause | What to investigate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Camera movement becomes uneven | Frame-rate instability | Graphics and FPS settings |
| Performance deteriorates during longer sessions | Thermal throttling | Device temperature |
| Enemy movement appears delayed or inconsistent | Network problems | Connection stability and ping |
| Game freezes or closes unexpectedly | Application or device instability | Updates, cache and available resources |
| Game refuses to install | Compatibility or Play Store issue | Android version, RAM and download connection |
| Insufficient storage message | Limited available space | Installed apps and downloaded resources |
| Some maps or audio are unavailable | Missing game resources | In-game Download Manager |

These observations are starting points rather than definitive diagnoses.

A game can experience network delay and frame-rate instability simultaneously.

Do not assume that every problem is caused by weak hardware.

## 2. Check your device compatibility

Activision publishes minimum Android compatibility requirements for Call of Duty: Mobile.

Its current support guidance lists:

| Requirement | Minimum |
| --- | --- |
| Android version | Android 6.0 or later |
| Physical RAM | At least 2 GB |

These are minimum compatibility requirements, not recommended specifications for consistently smooth competitive gameplay.

Meeting them does not guarantee that a phone will sustain high FPS or handle every graphics option comfortably.

Actual performance depends on:

- CPU and GPU capability.
- Available memory.
- Display characteristics.
- Thermal management.
- Android software and graphics drivers.
- Game updates and downloaded resources.

Before troubleshooting performance, confirm that your phone runs a supported Android version and has sufficient available storage.

See [Activision's official compatibility guidance](https://support.activision.com/cod-mobile/articles/call-of-duty-mobile).

## 3. Reduce graphics workload when FPS drops

If gameplay looks uneven or stutters during demanding scenes, investigate the graphics settings first.

Call of Duty: Mobile provides adjustable settings and multiple HUD options, but the exact available controls can vary.

### Start with a manageable graphics configuration

If your phone struggles with the current graphics quality, try reducing it.

Lowering visual quality can reduce the work required from the GPU.

The trade-off is less detailed graphics.

### Choose a realistic frame-rate target

A higher frame-rate option can make gameplay smoother when the phone can sustain it.

However, selecting the highest available option does not guarantee that the game will maintain that frame rate.

Try a lower supported target if the phone experiences repeated frame drops.

### Test changes individually

Use this process:

1. Record your current graphics settings.
2. Choose a repeatable multiplayer or training scenario.
3. Observe frame-rate consistency.
4. Reduce one graphics option.
5. Repeat the same scenario.
6. Keep the change only if the result is better.
7. Test again after a longer session.

Avoid changing graphics quality, FPS, display refresh rate and other phone settings all at once.

You need to know which adjustment actually helped.

## 4. Investigate overheating and performance deterioration

A phone generates heat while running Call of Duty: Mobile.

The CPU, GPU, display and other components consume power during gameplay.

If the device becomes too hot, thermal management may reduce performance to control temperature.

You may notice:

- Gameplay becoming less smooth after several matches.
- More frequent frame drops.
- Uneven movement during demanding scenes.
- A noticeably warmer phone.
- Reduced performance compared with the beginning of a session.

### What to do

**Allow the phone to cool**

Pause gameplay if the device becomes unusually hot.

**Reduce graphics workload**

Try lower graphics quality or a frame-rate target the device can sustain.

**Review charging behaviour**

Compare sessions with and without charging where practical.

**Avoid direct sunlight**

Do not add unnecessary environmental heat while gaming.

**Reduce unnecessary background activity**

Pause large downloads and other demanding workloads running alongside the game.

Allow the device to cool naturally.

Do not place it in a refrigerator or freezer.

Follow any temperature warnings displayed by the manufacturer.

## 5. Fix crashes, freezes and black screens

Activision's official troubleshooting guidance recommends a specific sequence for Android players experiencing crashes, freezes, black screens or problems launching the application.

Try these steps one at a time.

### Step 1: Restart your phone

Restart the device and open Call of Duty: Mobile again.

Test the game before changing anything else.

### Step 2: Update the game

Open Google Play Store.

Search for Call of Duty: Mobile and check whether an update is available.

Install the update through the official store.

### Step 3: Check for Android updates

Open your phone's Settings.

Look for System Update or Software Update.

Install available updates that are appropriate for your device.

Menu names vary between manufacturers.

### Step 4: Clear the game's cache

If the problem continues:

1. Open Settings.
2. Navigate to Apps or App Management.
3. Select Call of Duty: Mobile.
4. Open Storage.
5. Select Clear Cache.

The exact menu names may differ between Android devices.

**Important:** Clear Cache is different from Clear Data or Clear Storage.

Do not delete application data or reinstall the game without first confirming that your game account is properly linked and recoverable.

Activision documents these troubleshooting steps in its [official COD Mobile support article](https://support.activision.com/cod-mobile/articles/call-of-duty-mobile).

## 6. Fix installation and download problems

A failed download does not necessarily mean that your phone is incompatible.

Start by checking:

- Android compatibility.
- Available storage.
- Internet connection stability.
- Google Play Store behaviour.
- Pending app updates.

Activision recommends using a strong Wi-Fi connection when downloading or accessing Call of Duty: Mobile.

If Wi-Fi is unstable, investigate the router or try another reliable connection.

### If Google Play refuses to download the game

Activision also recommends checking the Google Play Store's cache and data when Android installation problems persist.

Before doing that, try:

1. Restarting the phone.
2. Checking the connection.
3. Confirming that sufficient storage is available.
4. Restarting the download.

If the problem continues, follow the Play Store troubleshooting instructions in Activision's support article.

Do not download modified APKs or unofficial installers to bypass an installation problem.

## 7. Manage storage and optional game resources

Call of Duty: Mobile contains more than its initial application installation.

The Google Play listing identifies optional HD resources, maps and other content downloads that allow players to manage the game's overall size.

This matters when your phone has limited storage.

### Check what is taking up space

Open your phone's storage settings.

Review:

- Installed applications.
- Large game downloads.
- Cached application data.
- Videos and screenshots.
- Other files you no longer need.

Remove content you no longer use, where safe.

### Understand optional downloads

Some game resources are downloaded separately from the initial application.

Before assuming that a map or feature is broken, check whether its required resources have finished downloading.

Use the game's available download controls and check that the selected content is installed.

### What if the game reports insufficient storage?

Activision recommends freeing space by clearing the cache of other applications, removing unused apps and deleting large files that are no longer needed.

Do not start deleting COD Mobile data randomly.

Protect your account access and downloaded content before performing more disruptive maintenance.

See [Activision's storage troubleshooting instructions](https://support.activision.com/cod-mobile/articles/call-of-duty-mobile).

## 8. Fix network lag and connection problems

Call of Duty: Mobile requires an internet connection to play.

A game that freezes during matchmaking, displays connection errors or produces unstable multiplayer gameplay may be experiencing a network problem.

Activision recommends a strong Wi-Fi connection for playing the game.

If Wi-Fi is unreliable, investigate the router and connection before changing the game's graphics settings.

### Try these checks

1. Restart the game.
2. Restart your phone.
3. Check whether other online games are experiencing similar problems.
4. Move closer to the Wi-Fi router.
5. Pause large downloads and uploads.
6. Test another reliable connection where available.
7. Check for official game-service notices.

### Why changing your registered region will not fix lag

Some players assume that changing their account's registered country will improve matchmaking or reduce ping.

Activision explicitly explains that players connect to servers closest to their physical location.

Changing the registered region does not change the server connection used for gameplay.

It is therefore not a network-performance fix.

Read [Activision's region-change policy](https://support.activision.com/cod-mobile/articles/call-of-duty-mobile-region-changes).

## 9. Understand what graphics and FPS settings can solve

Graphics and frame-rate settings address rendering performance.

They do not solve every form of lag.

| Problem | Should you start with graphics settings? |
| --- | --- |
| Uneven camera movement | Yes |
| Repeated frame drops | Yes |
| Performance deteriorates as the phone heats up | They may help, but temperature also matters |
| Delayed enemy movement | Investigate the connection |
| Matchmaking connection errors | No, investigate connectivity |
| Application crashes | Start with application troubleshooting |
| Insufficient storage | No, investigate available storage |

This distinction prevents you from wasting time changing visual settings when the underlying issue is elsewhere.

## 10. Are third-party FPS boosters worth installing?

Be cautious with applications advertising guaranteed FPS improvements, reduced ping or automatic COD Mobile optimisation.

A third-party application cannot upgrade your phone's CPU, GPU or physical RAM.

It also cannot guarantee a lower-latency route to the game's servers.

Avoid:

- Modified game APKs.
- Unofficial configuration files.
- Unsupported graphics modifications.
- Applications promising guaranteed FPS values.
- Tools requesting unnecessary sensitive permissions.

Use official game updates and device-supported gaming features.

If a performance tool claims to change protected system settings, investigate its permissions and actual capabilities before installing it.

## 11. What about playing COD Mobile through an emulator?

Activision's official support article states that Call of Duty: Mobile does not support emulator use.

This guide focuses on Android smartphones and does not provide emulator compatibility instructions.

Use the supported mobile application and official installation sources.

## 12. A practical COD Mobile troubleshooting checklist

When the game starts misbehaving, use this order.

**Step 1: Identify the problem**

Determine whether it is FPS instability, network lag, a crash, an installation problem or insufficient storage.

**Step 2: Restart the phone**

Test the game again before making further changes.

**Step 3: Check updates**

Update Call of Duty: Mobile and check for available Android updates.

**Step 4: Review graphics settings**

Reduce demanding settings when rendering performance is unstable.

**Step 5: Check temperature**

Allow the phone to cool if performance deteriorates during longer sessions.

**Step 6: Test connectivity**

Investigate Wi-Fi stability or compare another available connection.

**Step 7: Review storage**

Check available space and optional downloaded resources.

**Step 8: Clear cache**

Use the official cache-clearing procedure where appropriate.

**Step 9: Consult Activision support**

Check for known issues and follow official instructions before reinstalling the game.

## Final thoughts

Call of Duty: Mobile depends on the interaction between your Android device, downloaded game resources and internet connection.

A capable device can still experience network problems. A stable connection cannot compensate for a GPU that struggles with demanding graphics. A phone that meets minimum compatibility requirements is not guaranteed to maintain high FPS.

Identify the problem first.

Use official Activision troubleshooting guidance, keep the game updated and make controlled changes to your graphics settings.

The goal is consistent and responsive gameplay, not simply selecting the highest available settings.
