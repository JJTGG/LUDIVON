# Mobile Legends: Bang Bang on Android: Fix Lag, FPS Drops and High Ping

Mobile Legends: Bang Bang is a competitive 5v5 multiplayer online battle arena (MOBA).

Unlike a simple offline game, a typical MLBB match involves multiple players, hero abilities, minions, jungle monsters, defensive towers and large team fights.

Your phone must render the battlefield consistently while maintaining communication with the game servers.

When something goes wrong, you may experience FPS drops, delayed abilities, heroes jumping between positions, high ping or unexpected disconnections.

These problems do not necessarily have the same cause.

This guide explains how to investigate Mobile Legends performance and connection problems on Android without relying on unsupported graphics presets or third-party performance boosters.

## 1. Identify the problem first

Before changing settings, identify what is actually happening.

| What you notice | Possible cause | What to investigate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Camera movement becomes uneven | FPS instability | Graphics quality and frame-rate settings |
| Gameplay stutters during team fights | Rendering workload | Graphics configuration and device temperature |
| Abilities activate noticeably late | Network latency | Connection quality and network stability |
| Heroes jump between positions | Network instability | Ping, packet loss and connection interruptions |
| Ping suddenly increases | Congestion or routing problems | Wi-Fi, mobile data and background activity |
| Game disconnects during a match | Connection interruption | Network stability and reconnection |
| Game freezes or closes | Application instability | Updates, storage and temporary files |
| Matchmaking repeatedly fails | Connection or service problem | Internet access and official announcements |

These are diagnostic starting points, not definitive diagnoses.

A phone can experience graphics instability and network lag simultaneously.

Identify whether the problem happens:

- While moving around the battlefield.
- During large team fights.
- When several abilities are activated.
- During the loading screen.
- Only in online matches.
- Throughout the entire game.

The circumstances are important.

## 2. Check your Android device compatibility

Mobile Legends is distributed through Google Play by MOONTON.

The official listing is the appropriate starting point for checking whether your phone can install or update the current application.

Do not rely on unsupported minimum-RAM or processor tables from random websites.

Start with these checks:

- Confirm that the publisher is MOONTON.
- Open the official Google Play listing on your phone.
- Check whether your device is eligible to install the game.
- Review your Android version.
- Check available storage.
- Install the current version offered through Google Play.

Successful installation does not guarantee that your phone will maintain a particular frame rate.

A device may run the game but struggle with demanding graphics options or large team fights.

Actual performance depends on:

- CPU and GPU capability.
- Available RAM.
- Graphics configuration.
- Device temperature.
- Android software and graphics drivers.
- Game version and downloaded resources.

See the [official Mobile Legends Google Play listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.mobile.legends).

## 3. Understand FPS drops during team fights

Team fights are among the most visually demanding situations in a MOBA.

Several heroes can activate abilities at approximately the same time.

The battlefield may also contain:

- Multiple hero models.
- Skill animations and effects.
- Minions.
- Jungle monsters.
- Defensive towers.
- Other environmental elements.

These visuals create work for your phone's CPU and GPU.

If the device struggles to render the scene consistently, you may notice:

- Uneven animations.
- Sudden stuttering.
- Delayed visual updates.
- Camera movement that does not feel smooth.
- Performance deteriorating during larger engagements.

### What should you change?

Start with the graphics settings available inside your version of Mobile Legends.

If the device struggles with the current visual quality:

1. Open the game's settings.
2. Locate the available graphics controls.
3. Try a lower graphics-quality option.
4. Reduce demanding visual options where available.
5. Test the same type of gameplay again.
6. Compare performance during team fights.

Menu names and available controls can differ between game versions and devices.

Avoid applying a graphics configuration copied from another phone without testing it.

The best configuration is the one your own device can sustain.

## 4. Choose a realistic frame-rate target

A higher frame-rate option can make gameplay appear smoother when your phone can maintain it.

However, selecting the highest available option does not guarantee consistent FPS.

If the device repeatedly drops frames, a lower supported target may produce more consistent gameplay.

Consider the following:

**Display refresh rate**

A 120Hz display does not automatically mean Mobile Legends is rendering at 120 FPS.

The game must support the relevant frame-rate option, and the phone must be capable of sustaining it.

**Frame-rate consistency**

A frame-rate target that fluctuates significantly can make movement look uneven.

Do not select an option simply because it has the highest number.

**Power consumption**

Higher rendering workloads can increase power consumption and heat generation.

This can become important during longer sessions.

### Test one change at a time

Use this process:

1. Record your current graphics configuration.
2. Enter a practice or non-ranked match where available.
3. Observe movement and visual consistency.
4. Change one graphics option.
5. Repeat the same type of scenario.
6. Keep the change only if the result improves.

Do not change every phone setting and game setting simultaneously.

You need to know which adjustment actually helped.

## 5. Fix high ping and delayed abilities

High ping is different from low FPS.

Your phone may render the game smoothly while communication with the game servers remains unstable.

In a competitive MOBA, this can affect the timing of important actions.

You may notice:

- Skills activating later than expected.
- Heroes moving unpredictably.
- Delayed response to movement controls.
- Characters jumping between positions.
- Sudden changes in connection quality.

These symptoms may indicate network instability rather than a graphics problem.

### Check your Wi-Fi connection

If you use Wi-Fi:

- Move closer to your router.
- Avoid playing through an unreliable public Wi-Fi connection.
- Pause large downloads and uploads.
- Reduce unnecessary network activity on connected devices.
- Restart the router if connection problems persist.
- Test another reliable Wi-Fi connection where available.

A strong local signal does not guarantee a good route to the game server, but it helps eliminate weak Wi-Fi as a possible cause.

### Check mobile data

If you use mobile data:

- Check signal strength.
- Test from a location with more consistent coverage.
- Avoid areas where the connection frequently fluctuates.
- Pause background downloads.
- Compare results at another time.

Do not assume that a larger data plan automatically produces lower ping.

Download speed, latency and packet loss are different measurements.

### Avoid changing networks during a match

Switching between Wi-Fi and mobile data during an active match can interrupt communication.

Choose a stable connection before entering a ranked match.

Where possible, compare connections outside competitive gameplay.

## 6. Distinguish local network problems from wider service issues

Not every connection problem originates from your phone or router.

Sometimes the issue can involve service availability, routing or the game itself.

Use a simple comparison process.

**Check other online services**

Try another online game or service.

If several applications experience connection problems, investigate your local network or provider.

**Compare another connection**

Try a reliable alternative connection where available.

If one connection consistently performs better, investigate the weaker network.

**Check official announcements**

Review the official Mobile Legends news and announcement pages for relevant maintenance or service notices.

**Record the time of the problem**

If the connection issue appears only during certain periods, record when it happens.

This can help distinguish recurring local congestion from a wider service issue.

Use the [official Mobile Legends news page](https://www.mobilelegends.com/en/news/) and [patch-notes archive](https://www.mobilelegends.com/news/patch-notes).

## 7. Understand Mobile Legends' reconnection system

Mobile Legends advertises a Smart Offline AI Assistance feature.

According to its Google Play description, when a player loses connection during a match, the game can attempt to reconnect that player.

While the player is offline, AI can temporarily control the character to reduce the impact on the remaining team.

This is useful, but it should not be mistaken for a replacement for a stable connection.

A disconnection can still affect your contribution to the match.

The temporary AI behaviour does not guarantee that every interrupted connection will recover successfully.

### What should you do after disconnecting?

1. Restore your internet connection.
2. Return to Mobile Legends.
3. Allow the game to attempt reconnection.
4. Avoid repeatedly closing the application while reconnection is in progress.
5. Check whether the match has resumed successfully.

Do not deliberately disconnect to trigger temporary AI assistance.

The feature is a recovery mechanism, not an alternative way to play.

See the [official Google Play description](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.mobile.legends).

## 8. Investigate overheating and performance deterioration

Your phone generates heat while rendering a competitive multiplayer game.

The CPU, GPU, display and other components consume power during gameplay.

If the device becomes too hot, thermal management may reduce performance.

You may notice:

- More frequent frame drops after several matches.
- Stuttering during large team fights.
- A noticeably warmer phone.
- Performance deteriorating compared with the beginning of a session.

### What to do

**Allow the phone to cool**

Pause gameplay if the phone becomes unusually hot.

**Reduce graphics workload**

Try lower graphics quality or a frame-rate target your device can sustain.

**Review background applications**

Close applications performing unnecessary downloads or other demanding tasks.

**Avoid direct sunlight**

Do not add unnecessary environmental heat while gaming.

**Review charging behaviour**

Compare sessions with and without charging where practical.

Allow the phone to cool naturally.

Do not place it in a refrigerator or freezer.

Follow any temperature warnings displayed by the manufacturer.

## 9. Check game updates and downloaded resources

Mobile Legends receives game updates that can change heroes, maps, gameplay systems and other features.

Its official news and patch-notes pages document these changes.

For example, the S42 Starward Decade season announcement described changes to the battlefield, including updated map visuals and hero adjustments.

This matters because a game update can alter resource requirements and application behaviour.

When Mobile Legends starts performing differently after an update:

1. Record the current game version.
2. Check whether the game has completed its available downloads.
3. Confirm that Google Play does not show another pending update.
4. Review recent official patch notes.
5. Restart the game after updates have completed.
6. Test performance again.

If the game displays a resource-download or update-progress indicator, allow the required downloads to finish before entering a competitive match.

Avoid repeatedly interrupting large downloads.

See the [official Mobile Legends patch notes](https://www.mobilelegends.com/news/patch-notes).

## 10. Troubleshoot crashes and loading problems

If Mobile Legends freezes, closes unexpectedly or refuses to open, start with basic Android application troubleshooting.

### Step 1: Restart your phone

Close the game and restart Android.

Open Mobile Legends again and check whether the issue remains.

### Step 2: Check for game updates

Open Google Play Store.

Search for Mobile Legends and check whether an update is available.

Install updates through the official store.

### Step 3: Check Android updates

Look for available software updates in your phone's settings.

Menu names vary between manufacturers.

### Step 4: Check available storage

Review your phone's storage settings.

Remove unnecessary files or unused applications where safe.

### Step 5: Clear temporary cache where appropriate

On Android:

1. Open Settings.
2. Navigate to Apps or App Management.
3. Select Mobile Legends.
4. Open Storage.
5. Select Clear Cache, where available.

Menu names vary between Android manufacturers.

**Clear Cache is different from Clear Data or Clear Storage.**

Do not clear application data or uninstall the game without first confirming that you can recover access to your game account.

### Step 6: Check official announcements

If the problem started immediately after an update, review recent announcements.

A new version can introduce problems that require a developer-side correction.

Do not assume every crash can be fixed by repeatedly reinstalling the game.

## 11. Protect your account before reinstalling

Before performing disruptive maintenance, make sure you can access the account associated with your Mobile Legends profile.

Know the login method you use and confirm that you can recover access.

This matters because clearing application data or uninstalling a game can remove local application information and sign-in state.

Before proceeding:

- Confirm your account details.
- Make sure you can access the associated login method.
- Keep account credentials private.
- Do not share verification codes.
- Avoid third-party account-recovery websites.

If you cannot confirm that you can recover your account, do not begin by clearing application data or uninstalling the game.

Use the in-game Customer Service option for account-related assistance.

## 13. A practical Mobile Legends troubleshooting sequence

When Mobile Legends starts behaving unexpectedly, follow this order.

**Step 1: Identify the problem**

Determine whether it is FPS instability, high ping, a disconnection, a crash or an update problem.

**Step 2: Check device compatibility**

Confirm that your phone can install the official Google Play release.

**Step 3: Review graphics settings**

Try a more manageable graphics quality and frame-rate configuration.

**Step 4: Test team-fight performance**

Observe whether the problem occurs during visually demanding engagements.

**Step 5: Check device temperature**

Allow the phone to cool if performance deteriorates during longer sessions.

**Step 6: Investigate your connection**

Compare Wi-Fi and mobile data where possible.

**Step 7: Check updates and resources**

Confirm that the game is updated and required downloads are complete.

**Step 8: Restart the application**

Restart the phone and test the game again.

**Step 9: Protect your account**

Confirm that you can recover your account before clearing data or reinstalling.

**Step 10: Contact MOONTON**

Provide the device details, game version and exact circumstances of the problem.

## Final thoughts

Mobile Legends performance depends on the interaction between your Android device, the game's rendering workload and your connection to the game servers.

A phone that renders ordinary gameplay smoothly can still struggle during large team fights.

Likewise, high ping can delay abilities even when the visuals remain smooth.

The most useful approach is to identify the actual problem before changing settings.

Reduce graphics workload when rendering is inconsistent, investigate network stability when abilities are delayed, and protect your account before performing disruptive maintenance.

The goal is reliable gameplay and consistent team participation, not simply selecting the highest available settings.
