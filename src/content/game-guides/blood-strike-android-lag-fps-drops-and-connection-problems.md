# Blood Strike on Android: Fix Lag, FPS Drops, Crashes and Connection Problems

Blood Strike is a mobile battle royale shooter built around fast-paced gunfights, different combat modes and character abilities.

However, competitive gameplay depends on more than aiming accurately.

Your phone must render the game consistently, load the required resources and maintain a reliable connection during matches.

You may experience FPS drops when enemies appear, unstable performance during gunfights, high ping, matchmaking problems or application crashes.

These problems do not necessarily have the same cause.

This guide explains how to investigate Blood Strike performance and connection problems on Android using official NetEase documentation.

## 1. Identify the problem first

Before changing graphics settings or reinstalling the game, identify what is happening.

| What you notice | Possible cause | What to investigate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Camera movement becomes uneven | FPS instability | Graphics quality and frame-rate settings |
| FPS drops during gunfights | Rendering workload or game-side issue | Effects, graphics settings and game updates |
| Performance deteriorates during longer sessions | Thermal or resource limitations | Device temperature and background activity |
| Enemies appear to move unpredictably | Network instability | Latency and connection quality |
| Ping increases during matches | Network congestion or routing | Wi-Fi, mobile data and background downloads |
| Game freezes or closes | Application instability | Updates, storage and temporary files |
| Maps or resources fail to load | Incomplete downloads | Resource Download Center |
| Matchmaking repeatedly fails | Connection or service problem | Internet access and official announcements |

These symptoms are useful diagnostic starting points, not definitive diagnoses.

A phone can experience graphics instability and network lag simultaneously.

Identify whether the problem occurs in every mode or only during particular matches.

## 2. Check your device compatibility

Blood Strike is available for Android through Google Play.

Do not rely on unsupported system-requirement tables from random websites.

The official Google Play listing is the appropriate starting point for checking whether your device can install or update the current version.

Check the following:

- Confirm that the publisher is NetEase Games.
- Open the official Google Play listing on your phone.
- Check whether your device is eligible to install the game.
- Review your Android version.
- Check available storage.
- Install the latest version offered through Google Play.

Compatibility does not guarantee a particular frame rate.

A phone may install the game successfully but struggle with demanding graphics options or sustained high-FPS gameplay.

Actual performance depends on factors such as:

- CPU and GPU capability.
- Available RAM.
- Graphics configuration.
- Device temperature.
- Android software and graphics drivers.
- Game version and downloaded resources.

Use the [official Blood Strike Google Play listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.netease.newspike).

## 3. Fix FPS drops and inconsistent frame rates

FPS instability is particularly noticeable in a competitive shooter.

You may experience:

- Uneven camera movement.
- Sudden stuttering.
- Delayed visual updates.
- Frame drops when several players are nearby.
- Less consistent performance during gunfights.

NetEase has acknowledged that Blood Strike has experienced intermittent frame-rate problems across different devices and platforms.

In its July 2024 developer discussion, the team explained that some frame-rate drops were caused by intermittent bugs that required case-by-case investigation.

This is important.

Not every FPS problem is necessarily caused by an underpowered phone.

A game update can introduce or resolve performance problems independently of your hardware.

### Start with graphics quality

Open the in-game graphics settings.

If your device struggles to maintain consistent gameplay, try reducing graphics quality.

Lower visual quality can reduce the processing workload required to render the game.

The trade-off is reduced visual detail.

Avoid assuming that the highest graphics option is automatically the best choice.

### Review detailed animations

Blood Strike has previously introduced a Detailed Animation option in its graphics settings.

Its February 2024 update announcement stated that turning off Detailed Animation could improve frame-rate performance.

The exact availability and menu layout may have changed since that announcement.

Treat this as a documented setting from an older game version, not a guarantee that every current installation exposes the same control.

### Choose a frame-rate target your phone can sustain

NetEase introduced an Extreme frame-rate option for mobile players in July 2024.

The announcement described support for up to 120 FPS on eligible high-performance devices with high-refresh-rate displays.

However, this does not mean every Android phone supports 120 FPS in Blood Strike.

Three separate conditions matter:

1. The game must expose the frame-rate option for your device.
2. The phone's display must support the relevant refresh rate.
3. The CPU and GPU must be capable of sustaining the workload.

A 120Hz display does not guarantee that the game is rendering at 120 FPS.

Try a lower supported frame-rate target if the highest option produces repeated drops.

Consistency is more useful than selecting a high target that your phone cannot sustain.

See [NetEase's July 2024 graphics and FPS announcement](https://www.blood-strike.com/news/update/20240704/39377_1165000.html).

## 4. Understand FPS drops during gunfights

Blood Strike has previously received performance fixes for specific gameplay situations.

In its September 26, 2024 update announcement, NetEase documented fixes for:

- Frame-rate drops while moving.
- Frame-rate drops when enemies fired.
- Frame-rate performance around armour-break and recovery effects at low graphics quality.

This demonstrates why the situation in which an FPS drop occurs matters.

If performance becomes unstable specifically when moving, shooting or encountering several effects, record that behaviour.

Do not simply write that the game is lagging.

A useful report explains:

- Whether the drop happens while moving.
- Whether it happens when enemies fire.
- Whether it occurs during close-range fights.
- Whether it occurs in every game mode.
- Whether lowering graphics quality changes the result.

These details make the problem easier to investigate.

Read [NetEase's performance optimization announcement](https://www.blood-strike.com/news/update/20240927/39377_1183776.html).

## 5. Investigate overheating and performance deterioration

A phone generates heat while rendering a demanding shooter.

The CPU, GPU, display and other components consume power during gameplay.

When a device becomes too hot, its thermal-management system may reduce performance to control temperature.

You may notice:

- More frequent FPS drops after several matches.
- Uneven movement during longer sessions.
- A noticeably warmer phone.
- Performance deteriorating compared with the beginning of a session.

### What to do

**Allow the phone to cool**

Pause gameplay if the device becomes unusually hot.

**Reduce graphics workload**

Try a lower graphics quality or a frame-rate target that your phone can sustain.

**Review background activity**

Pause large downloads and other demanding applications while playing.

**Avoid direct sunlight**

Do not add unnecessary environmental heat while gaming.

**Review charging behaviour**

Compare sessions with and without charging where practical.

Allow the phone to cool naturally.

Do not place it in a refrigerator or freezer.

Follow any temperature warnings displayed by the manufacturer.

## 6. Fix high ping and connection instability

A shooter can render smoothly while the connection remains unstable.

This is why high ping should not automatically be treated as an FPS problem.

Possible network symptoms include:

- Delayed responses during combat.
- Unpredictable opponent movement.
- Sudden ping increases.
- Unexpected disconnections.
- Matchmaking errors.

### Investigate Wi-Fi

If you are playing through Wi-Fi:

1. Move closer to the router.
2. Check whether other devices are using substantial network capacity.
3. Pause large downloads and uploads.
4. Restart the router if the connection is unstable.
5. Test another reliable Wi-Fi network where available.

### Investigate mobile data

If you are using mobile data:

- Check signal strength.
- Test from a location with more consistent coverage.
- Avoid playing where the connection frequently switches between network conditions.
- Pause background downloads.
- Compare performance at another time.

### Disable VPN temporarily

If you use a VPN, disable it temporarily and test the game again.

A VPN changes the route used to reach online services and may introduce additional latency.

This is a diagnostic test, not a guaranteed fix.

### Compare connections

When possible, compare your usual Wi-Fi connection with a reliable mobile-data connection.

If the problem appears on only one connection, investigate that network first.

A connection with high advertised download speed can still have unstable latency or packet loss.

## 7. Distinguish network lag from graphics lag

These problems require different troubleshooting.

| Symptom | Start here |
| --- | --- |
| Camera movement stutters | Graphics and FPS settings |
| Performance drops during effects | Graphics workload and game updates |
| Phone becomes hot and performance deteriorates | Temperature and sustained workload |
| Enemies move unpredictably | Network connection |
| Ping repeatedly spikes | Network stability |
| Matchmaking fails | Connection and service availability |
| Game closes unexpectedly | Application troubleshooting |

Changing graphics settings will not repair an unreliable network route.

Likewise, switching from Wi-Fi to mobile data will not fix a GPU struggling with demanding visual effects.

Identify the actual symptom before changing settings.

## 8. Manage downloaded resources and storage

Blood Strike has expanded its resource-management functionality over time.

In June 2026, NetEase announced improvements to the Resource Download Center.

The update introduced:

- A Recommended Downloads page.
- Smart downloading.
- Resource cleanup functionality.
- On-demand EVO Skill resource downloads.

These features are important for players managing limited Android storage.

### Check your downloaded resources

Open the game's available resource-management section.

Review the resources that have been downloaded and identify content you do not currently need.

Use the game's resource cleanup functionality where appropriate.

### Avoid unnecessary downloads during matches

Large downloads can compete for network capacity.

Where practical, complete required resource downloads before entering a competitive match.

### Check Android storage

Open your phone's storage settings.

Review:

- Available storage.
- Large applications.
- Unused files.
- Old downloads.
- Other content that can safely be removed.

Do not delete application data randomly to free up space.

Make sure you understand what will be removed before using Android's storage controls.

See [NetEase's June 2026 Resource Download Center update](https://www.blood-strike.com/news/update/20260604/39377_1302862.html).

## 9. Troubleshoot crashes and loading problems

If Blood Strike freezes, refuses to open or closes unexpectedly, begin with basic application troubleshooting.

### Step 1: Restart your phone

Close the game and restart Android.

Open Blood Strike again and check whether the issue remains.

### Step 2: Check for game updates

Open Google Play Store.

Search for Blood Strike and check whether an update is available.

Install the update through the official store.

### Step 3: Check Android updates

Look for available software updates in your phone's settings.

Menu names vary between manufacturers.

### Step 4: Check available storage

Make sure your phone has enough free storage for the game and its downloaded resources.

### Step 5: Clear temporary cache where appropriate

On Android:

1. Open Settings.
2. Navigate to Apps or App Management.
3. Select Blood Strike.
4. Open Storage.
5. Select Clear Cache, where available.

**Clear Cache is different from Clear Data or Clear Storage.**

Do not clear application data or uninstall Blood Strike without first confirming that you can recover access to your game account.

### Step 6: Check official announcements

If the problem started immediately after an update, check NetEase's announcements.

A new version can introduce bugs that require a developer-side fix.

Do not assume every crash can be resolved by repeatedly reinstalling the game.

## 10. Check whether an update introduced the problem

Blood Strike receives frequent gameplay, content and system updates.

Its October 20, 2026 update introduced new gameplay content, map changes and other adjustments.

Updates can change the game's resource requirements and behaviour.

When a problem begins after installing an update:

1. Record the game version.
2. Identify what changed.
3. Check the latest official announcements.
4. Determine whether other players report a similar issue through official channels.
5. Avoid installing modified or older APK files from unofficial websites.

A problem that starts immediately after a game update may require a developer-side correction.

Do not downgrade the game through an unofficial installer.

Use the official Google Play release.

Read the [official October 2026 version update announcement](https://www.blood-strike.com/news/update/20261019/39377_1317020.html).

NetEase has previously provided a dedicated FPS Issue Report Form for players experiencing frame-rate drops.

The developer explained that reports help its team investigate performance issues across different platforms and devices.

When reporting a problem, provide useful details:

- Game: Blood Strike.
- Platform: Android.
- Phone manufacturer and model.
- Android version.
- Game version.
- Graphics-quality setting.
- Selected frame-rate option.
- Whether the issue occurs during movement, shooting or specific effects.
- Whether the phone becomes hot.
- When the issue started.

**bloodstrike@global.netease.com**

## 12. A practical Blood Strike troubleshooting sequence

When the game starts behaving unexpectedly, follow this order.

**Step 1: Identify the problem**

Determine whether it is FPS instability, network lag, a crash, a loading problem or a matchmaking issue.

**Step 2: Check compatibility**

Confirm that your phone can install the official Google Play release.

**Step 3: Review graphics settings**

Try a lower graphics configuration and a frame-rate target your phone can sustain.

**Step 4: Check device temperature**

Allow the phone to cool if performance deteriorates during longer sessions.

**Step 5: Test the connection**

Compare Wi-Fi and mobile data where possible.

**Step 6: Manage resources**

Check downloaded content and available storage.

**Step 7: Restart and update**

Restart the phone and install available official updates.

**Step 8: Protect your account**

Confirm that you can recover access before clearing application data or reinstalling.

**Step 9: Check official announcements**

Look for known problems affecting the current version.

**Step 10: Contact NetEase support**

Report the device, version and exact circumstances of the problem.

## Final thoughts

Blood Strike performance depends on the interaction between your Android device, graphics workload, downloaded resources and network connection.

Selecting the highest FPS option does not guarantee consistent performance.

Likewise, a fast internet plan does not guarantee stable multiplayer connectivity.

Identify the problem, make controlled changes and check official announcements when a problem appears after an update.

The goal is consistent gameplay, not simply selecting the highest available settings.
